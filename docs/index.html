Moodboard GitHub

Your components, on a wall, running.

A Mac app that plays every component of a Next.js project live on an endless wall. Move a dial and it rewrites your source.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/shariqmalik10/moodboard/main/scripts/install.sh | sh

Apple silicon, macOS 26 or later. Connecting a project needs Node 20.19 or newer. Free, open source, and it sends nothing anywhere.

Or download Moodboard.zip, or build from source on any Mac.

Works with Next.js, and optionally Paper and Claude Code. MIT licensed
